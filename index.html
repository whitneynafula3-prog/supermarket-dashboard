4. How It Works

  • Population vs. Sample Formulas: Population variance divides by N (1,800), whereas sample variance divides by n − 1 to provide an unbiased estimator.
  • Simple Random Sampling Without Replacement: Each draw selects n distinct customer transactions using a Fisher-Yates shuffle.
  • Sampling Variability: Repeated draws of the same size produce different sample means due to natural variation in basket sizes, while larger samples reduce standard error (σ / √n).
